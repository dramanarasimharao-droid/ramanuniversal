Terms and Conditions

Who we are. Raman Universal (ramanuniversal.com) is run by Raman Universal English Hub in Andhra Pradesh, India. We sell digital educational materials such as lesson PPTs, grammar decks, worksheets, question papers and PDFs for English, Class VI to X.

Using this website. By using this website or buying from it, you agree to these terms. If you are under 18, please buy with the help of a parent or guardian.

Products and prices. All prices are in Indian Rupees (INR). We may change prices or products at any time. The price shown when you pay is the price you are charged. We describe each product as clearly as we can, including what is inside and the file format.

Payments. Payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details.

Delivery. All products are digital. After a successful payment, the files are sent to the email address you give at checkout. Please check that the address is correct, and look in your spam folder if you do not see our email.

Ownership. All materials, designs, logos and videos on this website belong to Raman Universal. Buying a file gives you a licence to use it, not ownership of it. See Licence and Usage below.

Study aid only. Our materials help students learn and teachers teach. We do not promise any particular marks, grades or exam result.

Limit of liability. To the extent the law allows, our liability for any claim about a product is limited to the amount you paid for that product.

Changes. We may update these terms from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows the latest update.

Governing law. These terms follow the laws of India. Any dispute falls under the courts at Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh.

Privacy Policy

What we collect. When you buy or contact us, we collect your name, email address and, if you give it, your phone number. Razorpay shares the payment status and a payment reference with us. We never receive your card number, UPI PIN or bank password.

Why we collect it. We use your details to send the files you bought, confirm your payment, answer your questions and handle refund requests. We send news about new materials only if you agree to receive it.

Who sees it. We do not sell or rent your details. They are handled only by the services that run the shop: Razorpay for payments, Google for the website and email, and Payhip where a product is delivered through it. Each follows its own privacy rules.

How long we keep it. We keep order records for as long as needed for support, refunds and our accounts, and then delete them.

Your choices. You may ask us to show, correct or delete the personal details we hold about you, or to stop sending you updates. Write to us at the email below. This follows the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children. Students under 18 should buy with a parent or guardian, who then gives us the contact details.

Cookies. This website is built on Google Sites, which may use cookies and basic analytics to keep the site working.

Refund and Cancellation

No cancellation after payment. Our products are digital and are delivered right after payment, so an order cannot be cancelled once it is paid and the files are sent.

No refund after delivery. Because digital files cannot be returned, we do not give refunds once the files have been delivered or downloaded, except in the cases below.

When we will fix it or refund you. Write to us within 7 days of payment if any of these happen:

  • You paid but did not receive your files within 24 hours.
  • You were charged more than once for the same product.
  • The file is damaged, will not open, or is not the product you bought.

We will first send the correct file again. If that does not solve it, we will refund you.

How to ask. Email us at d.ramanarasimha.rao@gmail.com with your name, the email address you used and the payment reference from your Razorpay receipt.

How refunds are paid. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank. If a payment fails but money leaves your account, it is normally returned automatically by the bank within the same period.

When you buy a file, you get a personal licence to use it for learning and teaching. It is for one buyer and cannot be transferred.

You may

  • Study from the files yourself.
  • Show the PPTs in your own classes.
  • Print worksheets and question papers for your own students.
  • Keep a copy on your own devices for backup.

You may not

  • Sell, rent or give the files to others.
  • Post them on websites, Telegram or WhatsApp groups, YouTube or shared drives.
  • Remove the Raman Universal name or logo, or claim the work as your own.
  • Use them in paid coaching material or books without written permission.

Schools and institutions. If a school or coaching centre wants to share files across several teachers or sections, please ask us for a school licence before sharing.

Misuse. Copying or sharing the files without permission may break the Copyright Act, 1957, and we may take action.
